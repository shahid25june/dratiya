Who We Are

Gentle care for one of life's most personal journeys

R N S Fertility Clinic was founded with a simple belief: not every fertility challenge requires advanced medical procedures. Many couples struggle to conceive because of underlying imbalances — hormonal, nutritional, emotional or lifestyle-related — that can be corrected with the right support.

We combine herbal remedies, natural therapies, nutrition and lifestyle guidance into a personalised plan for each couple. Our goal is not just a positive pregnancy test, but a healthier body and a healthier pregnancy.

Every patient is treated with dignity, patience and complete confidentiality.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Atiya Sana

Fertility & Women's Wellness Specialist

Dr. Atiya Sana leads R N S Fertility Clinic with a deep commitment to natural, evidence-informed fertility care. Her practice focuses on understanding the root cause of infertility and treating the whole person — body and mind.

She works closely with both partners, guiding them through herbal treatment, diet, lifestyle correction and emotional wellbeing, so that couples feel supported and informed at every stage.

  • Specialised in natural & holistic fertility treatment
  • Female and male infertility care
  • PCOS, irregular cycles & hormonal imbalance
  • Pre-conception and pregnancy wellness guidance
