Hormonal Balance Restoration

Hormones control ovulation, egg quality, the uterine lining and sperm production. Even a small imbalance can make conception difficult. We use natural remedies, diet correction and lifestyle changes to gently bring your hormones back into balance.

  • Thyroid support
  • Prolactin balance
  • Insulin resistance
  • Progesterone support

PCOS / PCOD Management

PCOS is one of the most common causes of female infertility. Our natural PCOS programme combines herbal therapy, a tailored diet, weight management and activity guidance to improve ovulation and cycle regularity — without relying solely on hormonal medicines.

  • Ovulation support
  • Weight management
  • Insulin sensitivity
  • Cycle regulation

Irregular Periods & Ovulation Support

A regular cycle is a sign of a healthy reproductive system. We help regulate irregular, painful, scanty or heavy periods and support consistent ovulation, so you can better understand and use your fertile window.

  • Cycle tracking guidance
  • Natural ovulation support
  • Painful periods
  • Endometrium health

Male Infertility Treatment

Male factors contribute to a large share of infertility cases. Our natural therapies, nutrition plans and lifestyle corrections aim to improve sperm count, motility and morphology, along with overall vitality.

  • Low sperm count
  • Poor motility
  • Abnormal morphology
  • Vitality & stamina

Herbal & Natural Therapies

Carefully selected herbal remedies nourish the reproductive organs, support hormonal health and strengthen the body. Every formulation is chosen according to your individual condition and reports.

  • Personalised herbal plans
  • Uterine & ovarian health
  • Detox & rejuvenation

Fertility Nutrition & Supplements

What you eat directly affects egg and sperm quality. We create practical, easy-to-follow fertility diet plans and recommend supplements where needed to correct deficiencies and boost reproductive health.

  • Fertility diet charts
  • Vitamin & mineral correction
  • Healthy weight

Stress Management & Lifestyle Care

Chronic stress, poor sleep and an inactive lifestyle can quietly affect fertility. We teach relaxation techniques, breathing practices and realistic lifestyle changes that support both emotional wellbeing and conception.

  • Relaxation techniques
  • Sleep & routine
  • Exercise guidance
  • Emotional support

Pre-conception & Pregnancy Wellness

Planning a baby? Our pre-conception care prepares both partners' bodies for a healthy pregnancy, and we continue to guide you with diet and wellness advice once you conceive.

  • Pre-pregnancy check
  • Couple counselling
  • Early pregnancy guidance
